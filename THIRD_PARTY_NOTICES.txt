Hardware video dependencies in this preview:

- FFmpeg 7.1.1, System233 ffmpeg-7.1.1-r2 ARM64/x64 shared LGPL builds.
  SHA256-pinned archives are prepared by tools/PrepareNative.ps1.
  Video uses hardware D3D11VA only; software video pixel formats are refused.
  AAC/Opus audio decoding and PCM sample conversion use the native audio
  components, including dynamically linked swresample.
  Dynamic DLLs remain replaceable with ABI-compatible user-built versions.
